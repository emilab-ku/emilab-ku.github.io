Biography

Youngeun Kim is an Assistant Professor in the School of Electrical Engineering at Korea University and the Principal Investigator of the Efficient Machine Intelligence Lab. Before joining Korea University, Youngeun Kim was an Applied Scientist at Amazon AWS AI Labs, working on efficient multimodal LLM serving, and a Machine Learning Research Scientist at Meta Reality Labs, working on time-series neural networks for neuromotor-interface AR/VR applications.

Youngeun Kim received a Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Yale University in 2024, an M.S. from KAIST in 2020, and a B.S. from Sogang University in 2018. The lab's research spans efficient language and multimodal models, scalable agentic AI, model compression, continual adaptation, neuromorphic computing, and algorithm–hardware co-design.
